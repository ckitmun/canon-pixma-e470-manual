01 · START HERE

Getting started

Follow the basic checks before using the printer for the first time.

1Unpack

Remove packaging materials and protective tape. Keep the printer on a stable, level surface.

2Load paper

Open the rear paper support, adjust the paper guides and load compatible paper neatly.

3Install cartridges

Install the black and colour FINE cartridges following the on-screen or Canon setup instructions. Do not touch the electrical contacts or print head nozzles.

4Power on

Connect the supplied power cord, then press the ON button. Wait until the printer is ready.

First-time setup details can vary by device and region. Use Canon's official setup guide for the exact illustrated procedure.

03 · CONNECT

Connect to Wi-Fi

The E470 series supports connection through a wireless router and Wireless Direct. A router connection is generally recommended when available.

  1. Turn on the printer and make sure it is idle.
  2. Start the wireless setup process using the Canon setup instructions for your computer or smartphone.
  3. Choose your Wi-Fi network and enter its password when prompted.
  4. Complete the setup in the Canon software or app, then confirm the printer is available.
Wireless Direct connects a device directly to the printer without a router. Your device's internet connection may be interrupted while using this mode.

04 · EVERYDAY USE

Print, copy and scan

Print from a computer

  1. Install the correct Canon printer driver.
  2. Load paper and open the document.
  3. Choose Print, select PIXMA E470 and check paper and quality settings.
  4. Start printing.

Copy a document

  1. Place the original face down on the platen glass and align it with the guide mark.
  2. Close the scanner cover gently.
  3. Press Black for a black-and-white copy or Color for a colour copy.

Scan to a computer

  1. Install the scanner driver/software and connect the printer.
  2. Place the original face down on the platen glass.
  3. Open Canon IJ Scan Utility (or compatible scan software), select settings and scan.

Print from a phone

Connect your phone and printer to the supported network, then use Canon PRINT or a supported mobile printing option.

05 · KEEP IT WORKING

Maintenance

  • Keep the printer on a level surface and keep the ventilation areas clear.
  • Use the printer's maintenance functions, such as nozzle check and cleaning, when print quality becomes poor.
  • Replace cartridges with compatible FINE cartridges and follow the official replacement procedure.
  • For exterior cleaning, turn the printer off and unplug it. Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth.
Do not use alcohol, thinner, flammable sprays or liquids to clean the printer. Never touch the electrical contacts or print head nozzles.

06 · QUICK HELP

Troubleshooting

Printer does not turn on

Check that the power cord is firmly connected at both ends and try the ON button again.

Cannot print over Wi-Fi

Check printer and router power, confirm the Wi-Fi/network lamp status, and make sure both devices use the same network.

Print is streaky or faint

Check ink status, paper type and print settings. Run a nozzle check and the recommended cleaning procedure if needed.

Paper does not feed

Reload paper neatly, align the paper guides and check for jammed paper. Do not pull paper forcefully.

If the Alarm lamp flashes or the issue remains, check the error code/flash count in Canon's official FAQ before continuing.

07 · IMPORTANT

Safety information

  • Use only the specified power supply and supplied power cord. Do not damage, modify or excessively bend the cord.
  • Avoid overloaded outlets, wet hands when plugging in, and using the printer during lightning storms.
  • Keep the printer away from flammable solvents and do not spray flammable liquids onto it.
  • Unplug the printer before cleaning. Do not disassemble or modify the product.
  • If you notice smoke, unusual heat, strange smells, liquid spills or unusual noises, turn it off, unplug it if safe, and contact service.
This quick guide does not replace the complete safety instructions supplied with the product. Read the printed safety leaflet before use.
